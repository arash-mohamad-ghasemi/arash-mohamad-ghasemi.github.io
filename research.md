# Research Interests

My current research interests include:

- Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)
- Photovoltaic Power Systems
- Renewable Energy Integration
- Energy System Modelling
- Smart Energy Systems
- Heat Pump Technologies
- Industrial Decarbonization
- Techno-economic Assessment
- Multi-energy Systems
- Explainable Artificial Intelligence for Energy Systems
- Energy Flexibility
- Grid Stability
- Sustainable Energy Transition
- Digital Twins for Energy Systems
- Optimization of Distributed Energy Resources

## Current Research Direction

My long-term research vision is to develop intelligent, flexible, and sustainable energy systems by integrating renewable energy generation, battery storage, advanced control strategies, and artificial intelligence to support the transition toward carbon-neutral societies.
