# About Me

## Mohammad (Arash) Ghasemi

I am a research-oriented engineer with interdisciplinary expertise spanning renewable energy systems, battery energy storage systems (BESS), photovoltaic systems, mechanical engineering, and engineering management.

My academic background combines four master's degrees in Mechanical Engineering, Solar Energy Engineering, Electric Vehicle Engineering, and Business Administration. Throughout my studies and professional career, I have focused on integrating engineering knowledge with practical industrial experience to develop sustainable and economically viable energy solutions.

My research interests lie at the intersection of renewable energy technologies, energy system modelling, battery storage, photovoltaic integration, industrial decarbonization, heat pump technologies, techno-economic analysis, and data-driven decision support for future energy systems.

I am particularly interested in developing intelligent and resilient energy systems capable of supporting the Nordic energy transition through the integration of renewable generation, energy storage, and advanced operational strategies.

Beyond research, I have more than twenty years of engineering and EPC project experience in power generation, mechanical systems, quality management, and multidisciplinary engineering projects.

I am currently seeking doctoral research opportunities where I can contribute to interdisciplinary research addressing sustainable energy systems and industrial decarbonization.
